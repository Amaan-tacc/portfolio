What I do.

01

Design and visual direction

I design identities, guidelines, packaging and product visuals myself. That experience helps me give specific feedback and make practical creative decisions when I’m leading a team.

02

Client and team coordination

For QuickGrade, I worked between the client, one UI designer and two developers: clarifying requirements, coordinating revisions and checking workflows. For educational publishing, I directed two designers and a helper through briefs, page reviews and delivery.

03

Long-term client work

My work with education clients spans more than a year, across books, brochures, posters and banners. Returning work means understanding the context, carrying decisions forward and staying consistent as new needs arrive.
