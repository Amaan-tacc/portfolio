01 / Context

The brief.

A teacher with 50 students taking two tests a week can face 100 papers to assess and record. QuickGrade was designed to help manage handwritten-test uploads, grading, results and review in one platform. That workload is an illustration of the problem, not a measured project result.

02 / Decisions & responsibility

My contribution.

As the assigned project manager, I translated client instructions into feature lists, flows and explanations for the designer and developers. I coordinated client feedback through design and development, proposed organisation accounts early, checked workflows and followed issues through to fixes.

School and organisation accounts

I proposed organisation accounts early, so schools could manage teachers, students, classes and subjects within a linked structure.

Teacher review and corrections

The workflow includes student appeals and teacher review, correction and comments. Meaning-based grading still needed a clear human review path.

Upload and grading checks

I reported bulk-upload failures, incorrect grades and tests that uploaded without reaching the AI component, then followed fixes with developers and updated the client.
