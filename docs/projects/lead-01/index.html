01 / Context

The brief.

An ongoing education client needed books and communication materials across different programmes, subjects and student levels. The client was not a native English speaker, so the work began with careful clarification before it reached the team.

02 / Decisions & responsibility

My contribution.

I managed the client relationship, clarified briefs, assigned work to two designers and a helper, set visual direction and checked pages. I collected revisions and prepared delivery, while the team handled most of the book design execution.
