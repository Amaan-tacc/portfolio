01 / Context

The brief.

Coffee, teas, iced drinks and add-ons share a lot of information in a small space. The design needs to support a quick choice while keeping descriptions, prices and alternatives easy to find.

02 / Decisions & responsibility

My contribution.

I designed the layouts around clear drink families, using gold rules to group items and a consistent type hierarchy to separate names, descriptions and prices. Photography breaks up the lists and connects the pages, while repeated spacing and price alignment make the menu easier to scan.
