01 / Context

The brief.

Technical documents mix product benefits, prerequisites, architecture diagrams and instructions. Readers need to identify the purpose of a page and locate the relevant detail without losing the overall structure.

02 / Decisions & responsibility

My contribution.

I designed the page layouts to separate product context from detailed content, using headings, columns and diagram placement to establish a reading order. The selected pages show a concise product overview alongside more detailed setup material, with the supplied technical content and product identities retained.
