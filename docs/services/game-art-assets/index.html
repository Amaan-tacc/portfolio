How we work.

Our game artists bring experience from mobile game companies. We agree the style, asset list and technical requirements with your team, review the work in stages and prepare the agreed formats for handoff.

A useful starting brief.

Share the game concept, style references, engine, target platform and asset list. Include polygon budgets, texture sizes, rigging or animation requirements where relevant.
