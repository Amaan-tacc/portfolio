More about my work

I’m Muhammad Amaan, the person behind Amaanity. I’m hands-on with identity and visual design, and I also coordinate people and projects.

For educational publishing, I’ve directed two designers and a helper through briefs, page reviews and revisions. For QuickGrade, I worked between the client, a UI designer and two developers to shape requirements and keep delivery connected.

My work with returning education clients spans more than a year. That continuity is part of the work: understanding the context, keeping decisions consistent and helping the next brief move forward.
